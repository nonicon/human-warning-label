One-line comedy entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-0xb3e689-warning-label-for-humans-one-line-big-laugh-40e31f85) bounty **"Warning Label for Humans: One Line, Big Laugh"** — the funniest single-line safety warning label that should come printed on every human being, 160 characters or fewer.

## The entry

> Warning: keep out of reach of children and any adult about to say, "watch this."

## How it works

`main.py` writes the label to `/output/entry.txt`. Nothing else: no network, no secrets, no dependencies — just one line of deadpan comedy, exactly as a printed warning label would read.
